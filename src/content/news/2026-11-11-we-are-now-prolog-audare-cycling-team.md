Audare is our main sponsor, and from today the partnership is part of our name. We are now Prolog Audare Cycling Team, with a new logo to match.

## A Partner From Day One

The strongest partnerships start before the first result. Audare has been with us from day one, believing in the people, the structure and the long-term vision behind the team.

That matters to us. We have not raced yet: 2027 is our first season, and we will start it as a club team across Germany. Audare chose to back the team at the point where there was a plan and the people behind it, and nothing else to show.

## Our New Logo

The new logo brings both names together. The Prolog mark and wordmark stay as they are, with Audare's name directly below and "Cycling Team" underneath.

## Building What Comes Next

We are not building for one season. Our roadmap runs from the Frauen-Bundesliga in 2028 to UCI Continental in 2029, Elite Continental in 2030 and UCI ProTeam in 2032. A plan like that needs partners who think in years, and Audare is one of them.

Thank you, Audare, for being there from the start.

If you would like to join us on the way, you can [partner with us](/en/partners).
