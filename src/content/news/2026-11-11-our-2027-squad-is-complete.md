When we opened registration for Prolog Cycling, we didn't know how many riders would answer. The response went far beyond what we hoped for, and today we're proud to say our squad for the 2027 season is complete.

Thank you to every rider who applied. Reading about your racing, your training and your goals has been the best part of these first months, and it confirmed what we believed from the start: there is real depth in women's cycling, waiting for a team built to develop it.

## What Happens Next

Over the winter, the squad starts working together. Every rider gets an individual training plan, built on testing and performance data rather than guesswork, and we'll be sharing that journey as it happens.

- **Testing and profiling**: a performance baseline for every rider before the season starts.
- **Winter training**: structured plans, shared rides and our first team camp.
- **Racing in 2027**: our first season as a club team, racing across Germany at different levels.

## The Waiting List Is Open

The squad is full, but our door isn't closed. If you'd like to race with Prolog Cycling, you can still apply and join our waiting list. We'll get in touch whenever a place opens up, and we'll look at the list first when we plan the 2028 squad.

> "We're building this team in public, one step at a time. Every rider who applied is already part of that story."

Follow along on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/prologcycling/) for updates from the squad, and if you have any questions, [get in touch](/en/contacts).
